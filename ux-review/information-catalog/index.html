UX review / Room or category

Find it by room.
Or browse by category.

Your stay opens By room. Switch to By category whenever you prefer a topic-based list. Both views use the same information records.

  1. Start with a room.

    Your stay → Kitchen → Dishwasher.

  2. Change how you browse.

    Select By category while reading Dishwasher. Keep the same topic open, now under Appliances & entertainment.

  3. Return to its list.

    Back opens the category’s topic list. By room restores Kitchen; no category filter is left applied to the room view.

  4. Continue your stay.

    Help and the other main destinations stay available. Returning to Your stay keeps your catalog position during this session; a fresh page load starts By room.

Switching from a list

Show the other catalog’s last-used group, or its group index if it has not been used. Each mode remembers its own group during the session.

Switching from a topic

Keep the same topic ID and update its breadcrumb. For a topic shared by rooms, reuse the last matching room or use the first associated room. The guest can choose another room from the index.

Shared information

Whole cottage contains Wi-Fi, house rules and checkout. Outdoors covers amenity and safety topics. Neither is omitted from the room view.

Unknown information

Show an honest empty state or owner-confirmation note with Help available. Do not turn a missing manual into an invented instruction.

Catalog sample: 8 rooms and spaces, 7 categories, 14 shared topic records. Room labels and equipment associations follow the October 21, 2026 archive; current details and instructions need owner confirmation.
