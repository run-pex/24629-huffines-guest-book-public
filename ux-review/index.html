      On a phone

      A persistent bottom bar: Arrival, Your stay, Explore, Help. Detail content scrolls above it. The selected section has an underline and a programmatic current state.

      On a desktop

      The same four destinations move to the header. Your stay shows its topic links beside the content. Labels and order stay consistent.

      Go deeper, then return

      Breadcrumbs show the page’s place in the guide. Back returns to the last page visited. Both Help controls preserve this return path. Arrival, Your stay and Explore start a new section journey. Only detail pages have parent links. A named parent link opens its own section. Section landings have no parent breadcrumb; these tabs are peers.

      Open a link directly

      A shared link opens that topic immediately. A detail opened directly uses its parent for the in-guide Back action. A section opened directly has no in-guide Back control. All four sections remain one action away. In the final guide, normal browser Back follows browser history.

      Open catalog switching flow

      Your stay opens By room. Switch to By category whenever you prefer a topic-based list. Both views use the same information records.

      1. Start with a room.

        Your stay → Kitchen → Dishwasher.

      2. Change how you browse.

        Select By category while reading Dishwasher. Keep the same topic open, now under Appliances & entertainment.

      3. Return to its list.

        Back opens the category’s topic list. By room restores Kitchen; no category filter is left applied to the room view.

      4. Continue your stay.

        Help and the other main destinations stay available. Returning to Your stay keeps your catalog position during this session; a fresh page load starts By room.

      Switching from a list

      Show the other catalog’s last-used group, or its group index if it has not been used. Each mode remembers its own group during the session.

      Switching from a topic

      Keep the same topic ID and update its breadcrumb. For a topic shared by rooms, reuse the last matching room or use the first associated room. The guest can choose another room from the index.

      Shared information

      Whole cottage contains Wi-Fi, house rules and checkout. Outdoors covers amenity and safety topics. Neither is omitted from the room view.

      Unknown information

      Show an honest empty state or owner-confirmation note with Help available. Do not turn a missing manual into an invented instruction.

      Catalog sample: 8 rooms and spaces, 7 categories, 14 shared topic records. Room labels and equipment associations follow the October 21, 2026 archive; current details and instructions need owner confirmation.

      The structure

      Start with what the guest needs.

      Open full-size flow map

      One guide link before arrival; the same guide during the stay. Your stay is the everyday landing page; Arrival, Explore and Help are peer destinations. Each detail page offers its section parent and a way back and a visible Help link.

      1Arrive with confidence

      Starts when: Arrival link in pre-stay booking messages.

      Arrival & parking → Wi-Fi & connection → Your stay

      Success: Guest finds arrival guidance and settles in.

      Recovery: Cannot enter → Help → private booking messages.

      2Use the cottage

      Starts when: Guest needs an appliance or amenity.

      Your stay → Hot tub, pond & yard → House rules & dogs

      Success: Guest finds the relevant instructions and limits.

      Recovery: Instructions missing or equipment problem → Help.

      3Plan a day out

      Starts when: Guest wants something nearby.

      Explore nearby → Local place detail → Explore nearby

      Success: Guest checks official information before leaving.

      Recovery: Unverified option is withheld; return to the list.

      4Leave without surprises

      Starts when: Guest checks departure requirements.

      Your stay → Checkout → Your stay

      Success: Guest knows agreed requests and how to ask a question.

      Recovery: Time or requests unclear → private booking messages.

      5Resolve a problem

      Starts when: Help link from any page.

      Your stay → Help & safety → Wi-Fi & connection

      Success: Guest finds a relevant answer, or uses private booking messages for support.

      Recovery: Urgent and routine support need distinct approved guidance.

      A booking-message link and an on-site QR code are proposed entry points. A printed essentials sheet is a useful fallback for weak connections. Offline web access would need a separate implementation; it is not provided here.

      Research checked November 10, 2026

      What informed the plan.

      The sources below support the principles. The nine-page structure and the proposed journeys are our design recommendations, to validate with guests.

      Explain the things guests use

      Airbnb describes a house manual as a place for day-to-day instructions, with access limited to confirmed guests. Airbnb · House manuals

      In this guide: Use a House essentials page organized by equipment. Keep sensitive details in an approved private channel; this static guide is public.

      Keep checkout short and expected

      The November 3 update calls for reasonable additional rules and gives examples of acceptable checkout requests. Airbnb · House rules update

      In this guide: Do not automatically republish the archive’s dishwasher and outdoor cleanup tasks. Ask the owner to reconcile the requests before launch.

      Curate a small local guide

      Airbnb recommends brief, personal suggestions and regular accuracy checks. Airbnb · Local guidebooks

      In this guide: Use a short list by interest, with a detail page, official source link and verification date. No guessed hours or driving times.

      Make controls easy to tap

      WCAG 2.2 specifies a 24 × 24 CSS pixel minimum, subject to exceptions. Its enhanced criterion uses 44 × 44. W3C · Target size

      In this guide: Aim for at least 44 × 44 controls, generous spacing and visible keyboard focus. Keep important actions reachable without hover.

      Make the page easy to scan

      Semantic regions and logical headings help readers navigate and prioritize content, including with assistive technology. W3C · Page structure

      In this guide: Use clear page titles, short sections, a skip link and stable navigation. Keep critical warnings outside collapsed content.

      Make optional details keyboard accessible

      The disclosure pattern specifies keyboard activation and an exposed expanded or collapsed state. W3C · Disclosure pattern

      In this guide: Use native details / summary for secondary appliance explanations. Keep safety restrictions and core steps visible.

      Announce progress without moving focus

      Status messages should be available to assistive technology without requiring focus. W3C · Status messages

      In this guide: A future departure checklist should announce its progress count politely. Checkmarks are device-only progress, never a message sent to the host.

      Keep navigation predictable

      Repeated navigation should retain the same relative order across pages. W3C · Consistent navigation

      In this guide: Keep Arrival, Your stay, Explore and Help in one stable order. Detail pages keep their containing section selected; desktop uses the same labels as phone.

      Offer more than one route to information

      Providing multiple ways to locate pages supports different navigation needs. W3C · Multiple ways

      In this guide: Combine section hubs, contextual topic links and direct Your stay shortcuts. No mandatory search box or menu is needed for this small guide.

      Before a guest-facing launch

      The details we still need.

      These are editorial TODOs. Resolve them with the owner before replacing placeholders with guest instructions.

      Arrival & parking

      Confirm check-in time, parking capacity, access needs and verified directions. Choose a private method for exact location and entry details.

      Wi-Fi & help

      Confirm private credential delivery, troubleshooting steps, host contact channel and approved emergency guidance.

      House & hot tub

      Supply equipment models, short operating instructions, supply locations and current safety checks. Confirm the deck and old playset disclosures.

      Pool & fishing

      Resolve seasonal pool access versus the closed-pool description, and whether fishing equipment is supplied. Do not promise either yet.

      Rules & checkout

      Confirm current dog rules, checkout time, lock-up process and short requests consistent with the booking and current platform guidance.

      Explore nearby

      Select owner favorites. Verify hours, access, suitability, dog policies and map destinations with official sources before adding recommendations.

      Validate the flows with real guests. Ask someone unfamiliar with the cottage to find arrival information, Wi-Fi help, a pond restriction and checkout guidance on a phone. Watch for wrong turns and unanswered questions. These sketches have not been user-tested.
