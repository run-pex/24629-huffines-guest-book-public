variant: primary / secondary / outline / ghost. href produces an internal link. isDisabled prevents activation.

Interaction & motion. Hover deepens the fill and press deepens it further with a slight 98% press scale; buttons change color and never lift. Disabled buttons in every variant recede to a quiet-gray fill with stone text, no border or shadow, and no hover response. The selected example persists after motion ends.
