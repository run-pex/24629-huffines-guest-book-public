GuideBrandMarkPaletteGridGuideIconAnimatedGuideIcon

The Sunset brand symbol, shared color roles, spacing, radius, type and elevation scales, and the Lucide icon family. Specimens render with the live CSS variables.

Interaction & motion. Every icon inside a button, link or disclosure scales to 110% on hover or keyboard focus and to 94% on press. Eighteen icons load matching Lucide Animated artwork on first interaction; the other eight use brief one-shot glyph movement. Passive icons stay still; open-state chevrons retain their rotation. Reduced motion leaves static artwork and removes the movement.

Welcome & photography

WelcomePanelPhotoPanelGlassSurfaceGuidePhoto

Photo banners in three planes: a graded photograph, a scrim, and one liquid-glass capsule (GlassSurface) as the only top layer. WelcomePanel: stay / explore; title and body sit on the shadowed foot, and the capsule is the location or the link. Stay may supply a compact room title. PhotoPanel: photo, alt, optional caption capsule and icon. GuidePhoto uses curated source dimensions.

Interaction & motion. The photograph settles from 104% scale under a still capsule. The sticky Stay banner skips its container translation so its top edge stays exact. In Chromium the capsule gains the Glasswork rim lens (refraction) after hydration; other browsers keep the CSS glass. A linked capsule brightens its fill on hover and keeps the shared press scale and focus ring. Reduced motion removes the settle; reduced transparency and forced colors make the capsule opaque.

Room & category browser

StayBrowserCatalogSwitchCatalogRowCatalogGroupStayOfflineCatalog

CatalogSwitch keeps both room and category choices visible as text buttons. All rooms, including Kitchen, open in place. A selected room or amenity changes the banner photograph and supporting copy, while its heading stays the room name at one vertical position and left edge. Room, category, and exact item are encoded in the address; reload and browser history restore them. The final disclosure presents the no-JavaScript fallback.

Interaction & motion. Rows take a warm paper fill with ink-blue text on hover and do not lift. Their icons use the shared scale response. The banner pins to the viewport top, shrinks to one third over the next 0.7 banner heights of scrolling (at least 0.3 viewport heights), and fades supporting words until only the room title shows; scrolling up reverses it. Reduced motion switches between full and compact sizes at the pin point. Without JavaScript the original banner remains static.
